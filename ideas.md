## Option A: "Industrial Heritage" — Warm Brutalism

**Design Movement**: Warm Brutalism meets Heritage Craft

**Core Principles**:
1. Heavy typographic presence with Taviraj at large scale, commanding authority
2. Gold (#FEDA86) used as structural highlight — borders, dividers, and accent marks
3. Off-white (#F9F7F0) sections separated by thick charcoal (#3F4049) bands
4. Photography treated as full-bleed, dark-overlaid evidence of real work

**Color Philosophy**: Charcoal as the workhorse, gold as the reward. The palette communicates "we've been doing this since 1970 and we're proud of it." No pastels, no gradients — just honest, warm contrast.

**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric split-screen sections. Left-heavy text blocks with right-side imagery. Navigation anchored left. Stats and trust signals in horizontal bands.

**Signature Elements**:
- Thick gold left-border on section headings
- Full-bleed hero with dark overlay + large Taviraj H1
- Horizontal stats strip (charcoal bg, gold numbers)

**Interaction Philosophy**: Hover states reveal gold underlines on nav. Cards lift with subtle shadow on hover. CTA buttons shift from gold to saturated gold on hover.

**Animation**: Fade-in on scroll for section content. Subtle parallax on hero image. No gratuitous motion.

**Typography System**: Taviraj 74px for hero, 50px for section heads. Inter Tight 16px for all body. Gold accent on key numbers/stats.

## Option B: "Precision Engineering" — Structured Grid

**Design Movement**: Swiss Grid + Industrial Minimalism

**Core Principles**:
1. Rigid 12-column grid with visible structure
2. Charcoal and white as primary surfaces, gold only for CTAs
3. Typography-first: let Taviraj headings carry visual weight
4. Generous whitespace as premium signal

**Color Philosophy**: Restraint. White backgrounds dominate. Charcoal text. Gold reserved exclusively for the single most important action on each page.

**Layout Paradigm**: Strict column alignment. Services in 3-column grid. Hero with centered text on dark overlay. Alternating white/off-white sections.

**Signature Elements**:
- Sharp 0px border-radius on all interactive elements
- Thin gold horizontal rule separating nav from content
- Service cards with top gold accent bar

**Interaction Philosophy**: Minimal animation. Hover states are color shifts only. Clean, no-nonsense.

**Animation**: Entrance animations on scroll (translateY + opacity). No parallax.

**Typography System**: Taviraj for all headings. Inter Tight for body. Strict hierarchy.

## Option C: "Trusted Since 1970" — Warm Authority

**Design Movement**: Heritage Brand Modernization

**Core Principles**:
1. Dark charcoal hero with gold accent creates immediate authority
2. Warm off-white (#F9F7F0) sections feel welcoming and premium
3. Service cards use frosted/white surface with gold top accent
4. Trust signals (BBB, years in business, 24/7) prominently featured

**Color Philosophy**: The gold isn't decorative — it's the brand promise. Every gold element signals "this is what matters: call us, trust us, book us." Charcoal grounds everything in reliability.

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-width hero → trust strip → services grid → about split → testimonials → CTA → footer. Asymmetric within sections (text left, image right alternating).

**Signature Elements**:
- Gold announcement bar at top (free camera inspection offer)
- Hero: dark overlay on drain/pipe imagery, large Taviraj H1, gold CTA button
- Stats strip: charcoal background, gold numbers, white labels

**Interaction Philosophy**: Gold buttons animate to saturated gold. Nav links get cyan underline on hover. Cards get subtle shadow lift.

**Animation**: Smooth scroll-triggered fade-ins. Counter animation on stats. No excessive motion.

**Typography System**: Taviraj 74px hero, 50px sections, 24px subsections. Inter Tight 16px body, 14px labels.

## Selected Approach: **Option C — "Trusted Since 1970"**

This approach best serves Discount Drain's brand: family-owned since 1970, trusted local experts, with a clear value proposition (free camera inspection). The warm authority aesthetic matches the Contek design system perfectly while communicating reliability and approachability to homeowners and businesses in London, Ontario.
